## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-end, bespoke luxury beauty experience that honors the botanical richness, rituals, and prestige of African cosmetics. The aesthetic embodies tactile elegance, warmth, and quiet opulence. It appeals to a global, discerning clientele seeking clean formulations, regal heritage, and modern cosmetic sophistication.

The visual direction merges **Minimalist Editorial Luxury** with **Subtle Tactile Warmth**:
- Generous cream-toned negative space that treats each product as an objet d'art.
- Restrained, warm golden accents paired with deep, royal wine and burgundy tones.
- Meticulous micro-details: hairline metallic framing, luminous soft shadows, and graceful typographic hierarchy.
- Rejection of aggressive high-tech elements or cold corporate minimalism; every interaction feels ceremonial, pampering, and deliberate.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in ceremonial African botanical ingredients, raw bronze, and rich earth tones, balanced over luminous warm substrates.

- **Primary (`#5B1B29` / `#721C34`)**: Deep Imperial Wine and Burgundy. Used for prominent primary calls to action, high-level headers, and luxury accents. It conveys timeless heritage and prestige.
- **Secondary (`#C89D66` / `#D4AF37`)**: Warm Bronze and Polished Gold. Reserved for exquisite accents, active badges, premium borders, iconography, and decorative rules.
- **Tertiary (`#F3DDD7` / `#F7EAE8`)**: Warm Blush and Soft Rose Cream. Acts as secondary surfaces, container fills, and highlighting backdrops that bring human warmth and skin-like softness to the interface.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FCF6F5`)**: Soft Alabaster Cream. Replaces harsh pure white for default canvas backgrounds to protect the warmth and skin-toned ambiance.
- **Neutral Text (`#231815`)**: Deep Charcoal Umber. A softer, richer dark tone than standard black, delivering high contrast without clinical harshness.

## Typography

The typographical pairing creates an editorial rhythm between classical heritage and modern functional clarity:

- **Display & Headings (`Playfair Display`)**: Employs editorial proportions with romantic, high-contrast serifs. Used for hero moments, collection names, and narrative headers. Always styled with relaxed line heights to emphasize spaciousness.
- **Body & UI Elements (`Manrope`)**: A clean, balanced geometric sans-serif that ensures effortless legibility across descriptions, checkout flows, and technical skin ingredient lists.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Rendered in uppercase `Manrope` with deliberate tracking (`0.08em` to `0.14em`) to invoke luxury fragrance and haute couture packaging conventions.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system utilizes an open, fluid grid system framed by generous outer canvas margins:

- **Desktop (12-column fluid grid)**: Max content boundary capped at 1440px with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Editorial hero sections and product galleries use asymmetrical offsets (e.g., 5-column product presentation paired with 7-column imagery) to mimic luxury print publications.
- **Tablet (8-column fluid grid)**: `2rem` margins, `1.25rem` gutters. Product grids step down naturally to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (4-column fluid grid)**: `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Full-bleed carousel moments with subtle margin peeks give tactile preview of neighboring imagery.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Vertical pacing favors airy intervals (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) between narrative sections, ensuring components never feel compressed or rushed.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is communicated through warm tonal layering and soft, diffused ambient light rather than harsh structural drop shadows:

- **Surface Tonal Stacking**: Depth is primarily established through soft shifts in surface warmth:
  - Base canvas: `#FCF6F5` (Soft Alabaster)
  - Resting cards & flyouts: `#FFFFFF` or `#F7EAE8`
  - Floating panels & overlays: `#FFFFFF` with hairlines
- **Warm Ambient Shadows**: Shadows are tinted with deep wine-bronze undertones rather than cold neutral gray:
  - *Resting Card Elevation*: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(91, 27, 41, 0.04)`
  - *Hover Elevation*: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(91, 27, 41, 0.08)`
  - *Modal & Drawer Elevation*: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(35, 24, 21, 0.12)`
- **Hairline Golden Framing**: Interactive containers and card edges feature subtle 1px borders tinted in semi-transparent warm bronze (`rgba(200, 157, 102, 0.25)`), creating an illuminated, jewel-box finish.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, tailored shape language (`Soft` roundedness). 

- Standard inputs, buttons, and product tiles use subtle `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) corner radials, retaining architectural precision while softening rigid geometric edges.
- Circular shapes are strictly reserved for avatar portraits, shade swatches, and small iconography discs.
- Pill shapes are avoided for primary buttons to prevent a juvenile or tech-app aesthetic; clean, structured rectangles with soft radii preserve prestige.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#5B1B29` background with `#FCF6F5` text, subtle 1px border in `#721C34`, padded with `1rem 2rem`. Text set in uppercase `label-lg`. On hover, transitions smoothly to a lighter burgundy (`#721C34`) with a soft wine ambient glow.
- **Secondary / Luxury Accent**: Transparent fill with a 1px border in `#C89D66`, text in `#5B1B29`. On hover, fills with `#F7EAE8` blush tone.
- **Tertiary (Ghost)**: Pure text with uppercase tracking and an underline offset by 4px in `#C89D66`.

### Product Cards
- Built on a `#FFFFFF` foundation with a 1px border in `rgba(200, 157, 102, 0.2)`. 
- Images feature a 4:5 editorial aspect ratio with warm cream backing.
- Top badge accommodates ritual descriptors (e.g., "COLD-PRESSED OIL", "HERITAGE EDITION") using `label-sm` in `#721C34` over `#F3DDD7`.
- Price displayed in `Manrope` Medium, product name in `Playfair Display`.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Background filled with `#FFFFFF`, bordered by 1px `rgba(35, 24, 21, 0.15)`.
- On focus, border transitions to `#C89D66` with a gentle `0 0 0 1px #C89D66` ring; no aggressive blue default rings.
- Floating labels in `label-md` uppercase using `#5B1B29`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Subtle capsule styling: `#FCF6F5` fill with `rgba(200, 157, 102, 0.35)` border.
- Selected state activates `#5B1B29` fill with `#FCF6F5` text, providing immediate, distinct editorial clarity.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square/circle controls with a 1px border in `#C89D66`.
- Checked state fills with `#5B1B29` and displays an inner gold pip or white icon checkmark.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Shade & Texture Swatches**: Circular tiles displaying high-resolution cosmetic macro textures (creams, pigments, oils) bordered with a 2px offset ring in `#D4AF37` upon selection.
- **Ingredient Provenance Accordion**: Minimalist expandable rows framed with 1px `#F3DDD7` dividers, highlighting African botanical sourcing notes in `Playfair Display` subtitles paired with standard body text.
